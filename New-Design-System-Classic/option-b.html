The Process

How limb lengthening works.

Phase 01
01

Consultation & Planning

Confidential in-person or virtual consultation. Dr. Basmajian evaluates your health, discusses realistic expectations, and creates a personalized surgical plan.

Phase 02
02

Surgery & Lengthening

A Precice internal nail is surgically placed. Over 3–4 months, the nail gradually lengthens your bone by ~1mm per day using an external magnetic remote. No visible hardware.

Phase 03
03

Recovery & Results

Physical therapy begins immediately. Most patients return to daily activities within 3–4 months and full activity by 6–12 months. Final height gain: 3–6 inches.

Am I a Candidate?

Who is a good candidate for limb lengthening?

  • 01Generally healthy adults aged 18–55 (older patients evaluated case-by-case).
  • 02Non-smoker or willing to quit 6 weeks before surgery.
  • 03BMI under 35 (ideal under 30).
  • 04No active bone disease or uncontrolled diabetes.
  • 05Able to commit to 3–6 months of recovery and physical therapy.
  • 06Realistic expectations about height gain (2–3″ per bone, up to 6″ combined).
  • 07Cosmetic height enhancement OR limb-length discrepancy correction.
  • 08Revision patients: previous surgery complications or unsatisfactory results.
Not sure if you qualify?

Many patients told “no” elsewhere are candidates here.

The best way to find out is through a confidential consultation. Dr. Basmajian evaluates each patient individually — many patients who were told "no" by other surgeons are candidates at our practice due to our trauma reconstruction expertise.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

01 How much does limb lengthening surgery cost? +
Femur lengthening starts at $77,500 (Precice 2.2) or $80,000 (Precice MAX). Tibia lengthening ranges from $83,000–$85,000. Every quote includes surgery, implant, anesthesia, hospitalization, and follow-up care. Financing available through CareCredit.
02 How much height can I gain? +
Femur lengthening typically adds 2–3 inches. Tibia lengthening adds another 2–3 inches. Combined staged procedures can achieve up to 5–6 inches total. Results are permanent.
03 How long is the recovery? +
Active lengthening takes 3–4 months. Most patients return to daily activities within 3–4 months and full activity by 6–12 months.
04 Will there be visible scars or hardware? +
The Precice system is entirely internal — no external frames, no visible hardware. Small incisions heal to minimal scars.
05 Do you accept out-of-state & international patients? +
Yes — our concierge program coordinates flights, ground transportation, extended-stay housing, and physical therapy. We've served patients from 50+ states and countries.
06 Can you handle revision cases? +
Yes. Dr. Basmajian's trauma reconstruction expertise means we accept revision cases many surgeons decline. We will review your history and imaging before committing to any plan.
