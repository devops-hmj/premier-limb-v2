02·b

The index marker.

§ 02b · Spec

Pillars, list rows, candidate items, plate captions, and section headers are marked with italic serif Arabic numerals, zero-padded to two digits. The numeral takes Premier Blue; the row content takes Ink. The lowercase Roman numerals (i, ii, iii…) are retired — they're fussy past iv and don't scale.

Standard · Editorial Numerals

Italic serif Arabic numerals, zero-padded.

Newsreader italic at 28px for primary list markers (candidate items, concierge rows, plate captions). Scales down to 22px for trust strips and 20px for inline tags. Always in Premier Blue. Numbers above 99 fall back to three-digit padding (100, 101).
01Generally healthy adults aged 18–55.
02Non-smoker or willing to quit 6 weeks before surgery.
03BMI under 35 (ideal under 30).
04No active bone disease or uncontrolled diabetes.
05Able to commit to 3–6 months of recovery and physical therapy.
Do · Don't Do zero-pad single digits (01, 02) for editorial rhythm. Do keep numerals in italic Newsreader and Premier Blue. Don't mix with lowercase Roman numerals — they're retired. Don't use the marker as decoration; if a list has no inherent order, drop it.

04

The voice.

§ 04 · Tone
01

Editorial, not promotional.

We write like a periodical, not a brochure. Decks, captions, datelines, foliage. The reader is treated as an intelligent adult, not a customer to be persuaded.

02

Evidenced, not asserted.

Every claim earns its place with a number, a credential, or a named patient. "Hundreds of procedures." "Pomona Valley Hospital." "Marcus T., San Francisco." Specificity is the proof.

03

Direct, not theatrical.

Short sentences. Active voice. No hedging, no superlatives, no urgency theatre. The Premier blue speaks volume; the language stays measured.

Say this

Aligned with the voice
  • 01"Confidence you can stand behind."
  • 02"Hundreds of procedures performed."
  • 03"Cases other surgeons won't take on."
  • 04"Confidential consultation."
  • 05"Results are permanent."

Never this

Breaks the voice
  • 01"World-class!" / unsubstantiated superlatives
  • 02"Transform your life today!" / urgency theatre
  • 03"#1 surgeon in California" / unverifiable claims
  • 04"Limited time offer!" / discounts cheapen the brand
  • 05Emoji. Exclamation marks. Hashtags. ALL CAPS sentences.
