How to Read These Results

Three ways we show the change.

Limb lengthening is not a before/after you judge from one photo. The honest evidence lives in three places, and we present all three so you can weigh the outcome the way a surgeon does.

01 · Stature

Height & proportion

Standing comparisons captured against a fixed scale. The goal is a result that matches your natural proportions, so the change reads as "you look great," not "something looks off." [ Placeholder copy. ]

02 · Radiographic

The bone on film

X-rays are the clinical proof. They show the internal Precice nail in place, new regenerate bone forming through distraction, and full consolidation once healed. Non-identifying and objective.

03 · Function

Gait & movement

For many patients, especially revision cases, the real result is walking normally again. Before and after comparisons document range of motion and gait once physical therapy is complete. [ Placeholder copy. ]

Our Commitment

How we handle your privacy.

Discreet by Default

Your story is yours to share.

We are never performative about patient results. Nothing appears here without your explicit written permission, and choosing to stay private never changes the care you receive. [ Confirm final policy language with counsel and Dr. Basmajian. ]

01

Written consent, every time

No photo, X-ray, or story is published without a signed release specific to marketing use. Consent can be withdrawn.

02

Anonymous by choice

Faces and identifying details are removed unless a patient specifically approves them. Radiographic images are non-identifying by nature.

03

Honest presentation

Images are not retouched to exaggerate results. Before and after are captured at the same pose, distance, and scale.

04

HIPAA aligned

Protected health information is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices. Marketing use is handled separately and only with consent.
