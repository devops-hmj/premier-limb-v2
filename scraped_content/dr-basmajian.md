Meet Dr. Hrayr Basmajian
========================

Dr. Basmajian is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon specializing in trauma and non-trauma musculoskeletal concerns. He is the Orthopedic Trauma Medical Director at Pomona Valley Hospital Medical Center, one of the busiest Trauma Centers in Los Angeles, and an assistant professor of orthopedic surgery. Previously, he was Chair of Orthopedic Trauma at Loma Linda University Medical Center.

Dr. Basmajian’s career has been punctuated by an insatiable drive toward quality improvement in the orthopedic programs he helms as well as always striving to hone his own skills. His knowledge, compassion, and deep understanding of orthopedic surgery and patient needs have made him a go-to provider across Southern California and the United States.

Dr. Basmajian has developed a particular interest in limb lengthening for several reasons, not least of which is the stunning transformation a patient undergoes physically and emotionally. Further, as a trauma surgeon, safe and effective limb lengthening is a cornerstone of his practice, with several hundred of these procedures performed successfully over his time in practice. Dr. Basmajian has also seen the effects of improper limb lengthening that can result in deformity and a need for costly and painful correction.

**Doing it right the first time is an integral part of his practice.**

_The need for quality care in orthopedic surgery, limb lengthening, and medicine in general is as significant today as ever. At Premier’s Limb-lengthening Institute, each patient is integral to our practice and deserves the utmost in attention and care. In addition to his private practice endeavors, Dr. Basmajian trains other surgeons in the most advanced and newest orthopedic techniques to improve outcomes and decrease complications. He works with various surgical programs and medical centers to enhance quality of care through novel operating room procedures and setups. He also lectures regularly, teaching residents and seasoned orthopedic surgeons about the art of trauma care in this ever-changing field.
